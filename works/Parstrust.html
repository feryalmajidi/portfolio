Project overview

As a UI/UX designer, my objective was to create a user-friendly and efficient cryptocurrency trading app that simplifies the process of buying, selling, and managing digital assets. I focused on the needs of the users, ensuring that they could easily access essential information, view real-time market data, and execute trades with minimal effort. By prioritizing a seamless user experience, the CryptoTrade app design provides a streamlined and secure platform for both novice and experienced traders.

Problem

Cryptocurrency platforms often overwhelm users with charts, numbers, unfamiliar terminology, and multiple actions competing for attention.

Solutions

  • Pars Trust was designed to make digital finance feel as simple as everyday banking.
  • The experience combines a clean visual hierarchy with intuitive navigation, allowing users to quickly understand.

User persona

Pain points

  • Finds most crypto apps overly complex and intimidating for beginners
  • Struggles to track overall portfolio performance across multiple assets
  • Distrusts platforms that lack clear explanations for investment recommendations

Motivation

  • Wants to grow wealth through crypto without spending hours learning complex systems
  • Needs a reliable platform with transparent powered insights
  • Prefers a clean, distraction-free interface with clear visual cues

Before moving into full screens, I put together a lightweight design system — color, type, and reusable component states — so the interface stayed consistent across every flow instead of being rebuilt screen by screen.

Visual design

The app's visual design was carefully crafted to provide a clean and modern interface that aligns with the app's overall goals. We chose a minimalistic design with bright accent colors and creative cards to ensure an intuitive user experience.

Onboarding

Designed to guide you seamlessly through the app's essential functions, you’ll learn how to sign up, create your profile, and experience the convenience of managing your crypto investments. Discover how easy it is to navigate the world of cryptocurrency with our user-friendly platform

Authorization & Verification

Our onboarding and registration process is designed to ensure maximum security while maintaining a smooth user experience. Users can easily create and secure their accounts through a guided, step-by-step flow:

  • Account Creation & Verification: Fast sign-up using social media accounts (Google, Facebook, X) or email with OTP verification, followed by basic profile setup and preference profiling to tailor the experience.
  • Identity Verification (KYC): Hassle-free document scanning (ID Card or Passport) with real-time camera guidance to fulfill compliance standards securely.
  • Security & Personalization: Multi-layer security setup, including terms acceptance, custom PIN creation, biometric authentication (Fingerprint), and immediate account readiness.

Home / Dashboard

The dashboard is designed with a user-centric approach, ensuring that the Crypto App is not only functional but also easy to navigate. By incorporating user feedback and iterative testing, we have created a streamlined interface with a clear visual hierarchy. Robust security features are also integrated to instill confidence in users regarding their digital transactions, ensuring a safe and reliable trading environment.
